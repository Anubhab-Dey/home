On the texts and their rights

These are older English translations and commentaries, not modern editions by contemporary publishers. The works and historical translations are believed to be in the public domain in India under the normal copyright term. Source files retain their own Project Gutenberg notices and licence. We provide them unchanged as text downloads and render them in an accessible reading layout.

Do not confuse an older translation with a sampradāya's authoritative commentary. For study and sādhana, compare the original Sanskrit and a trusted traditional commentary. No translations or interpretations are silently represented as the original.

For the original Sanskrit Bhagavad Gita, see the Sanskrit Wikisource text ↗. This is a separate external primary-text reference, not part of the locally hosted English editions.
