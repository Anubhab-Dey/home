About this library · Included for study, not endorsement

This collection is provided for reading, preservation and study. Including a scripture, translation, commentary or historical edition does not mean that I personally agree with or endorse every passage, interpretation, claim or prescribed practice it contains. Original texts, translators' interpretations and editorial notes are distinct, and different editions or traditions may differ. Please consult each work's attribution and edition-specific warnings.

Historical discussions of medicine, law and astrology are presented as source literature, not as modern medical or legal advice or verified scientific conclusions.

05 / READING WITH CARETEXT • TRANSLATION • TRADITION

Know what you're reading.

01

Mūla is not a translation.

The original Sanskrit or Bengali work, a modern transcription, an English rendering and a sampradāya's commentary are separate editorial layers. We label them rather than collapse them.

02

Old doesn't mean infallible.

These are historical translations, not endorsements of every interpretation. Recensions, śloka numbering, textual errors and translator choices require checking against the source.

03

Free access, clear rights.

We host the historical editions we can justify. External links do not imply permission to mirror modern translations, digitizations or publisher commentaries.
