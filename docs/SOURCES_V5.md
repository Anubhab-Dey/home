# Scriptures V5: edition provenance, limits, and safety

## On-site full-text readers (historical English)

- **Yoga Sūtras of Patañjali**, Charles Johnston (1912): Project Gutenberg #2526, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2526. Johnston’s historical interpretation includes introductions and commentary. It is not a complete Sanskrit-English parallel edition; interpretive perspective is editorial.
- **Yājñavalkya Smṛti · historical translation**, W. A. Montriou and E. Röer (nineteenth century): Project Gutenberg #21918, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/21918. Historical English translation titled Hindu Law and Judicature from the Dharma-Śāstra; do not assume this is the complete Sanskrit work. Historical legal prescriptions are not current law.
- **Harivaṃśa**, Manmatha Nath Dutt (historical): Project Gutenberg #61937, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/61937. A prose English edition, not a śloka-aligned Sanskrit-English translation. Numbering may vary by recension.
- **Sarva-darśana-saṃgraha**, E. B. Cowell and A. E. Gough (historical): Project Gutenberg #34125, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/34125. A historical survey from a particular philosophical standpoint. The translation does not include a verified Sanskrit parallel edition.
- **Vedānta Sūtras · Śaṅkara, Vol. I**, George Thibaut (1890s): Project Gutenberg #16295, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/16295. This is SBE Volume I, one part of Thibaut’s historical English translation of Śaṅkara’s Advaita commentary, not the entire series or a neutral/Gauḍīya interpretation.

## Original scans (PDF, hosted as independent exact files)

- **Manu-smṛti · Bühler English**: [Wikimedia Commons source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_sacred_books_of_the_East_(IA_1922707.0025.001.umich.edu).pdf); Georg Bühler · Sacred Books of the East, Vol. XXV. Historical source for textual and legal-history study. Caste, gender and punishment provisions should not be confused with present law, universal practice, or a single binding theological verdict.
- **Manu-smṛti · Sanskrit print**: [Wikimedia Commons source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Manu_Smriti_(IA_in.ernet.dli.2015.550803).pdf); Historical printed Sanskrit edition, Keshav Prasad Sharma. Separate print witness from the English translation; their pagination, commentaries and readings are not automatically aligned.
- **Bṛhajjātaka · Varāhamihira**: [Wikimedia Commons source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Brihat_jataka_of_Varaha_Mihira_(IA_b2488442x).pdf); N. Chidambaram Iyer · Foster Press. A historical horoscopy text, not an empirically validated predictive model. This scanned translation is not paired śloka-for-śloka with the Sanskrit.
- **Sūrya Siddhānta**: [Wikimedia Commons source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Translation_Of_The_Surya-siddhanta_(IA_in.ernet.dli.2015.96668).pdf); Ebenezer Burgess · American Oriental Society. Historical astronomical calculations and interpretive choices are edition-dependent; not a substitute for modern astronomical measurement. Not a Sanskrit-English parallel edition.
- **Suśruta Saṃhitā · Sanskrit edition**: [Wikimedia Commons source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sushruta_Samhita_(IA_in.ernet.dli.2015.405963).pdf); Historical Sanskrit scan · Digital Library of India. An old Sanskrit printed witness; the catalogue year is unverified and the scan has not been philologically reviewed. Historical treatment and surgery are not modern medical advice. No English parallel is claimed.

## Editorial and licensing boundaries

- Each reader contains the full Project Gutenberg source text including its original license. The texts are public-domain-era translations; Gutenberg's trademark and redistribution terms still matter.
- Wikimedia Commons original scans are checked against file metadata and not transformed. Check the individual Commons rights record and any jurisdiction-specific limits before downstream commercial reuse.
- A source PDF displayed through the browser's embedded PDF viewer is an **on-site facsimile reader**, not a retyped/searchable Sanskrit verse reader.
- The two Manu-smṛti PDFs are different witnesses, *not* aligned; no verse-matching software is claimed.
- The Suśruta scan is a historical **Sanskrit-only** printed witness, with publication year taken from an unverified catalogue record. It is not an English translation or verified clinical guidance.
- This release does not bulk-copy SanskritDocuments/GRETIL, modern translations, or copyrighted Gauḍīya commentaries.
- The old medical texts describe historical theory and therapies; they are not medical advice. The horā text describes traditional astrology and is not empirically confirmed forecasting.
- No Sanskrit original is claimed for the HTML English-only texts.
- No new material is written into GitHub; deploy first, then reconcile source files into the personal repo separately.
